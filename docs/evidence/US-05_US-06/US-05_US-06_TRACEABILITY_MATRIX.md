## Known gaps and alignment decisions

1. **Completion versus final self-review:** `CompletionServiceImpl` currently requires each required task to be both finalized and reviewed when `ORDERED_SELF_REVIEW` is active. Frozen DR2/BR-06 wording says self-review gates next-task progression and final review may be tracked separately. Keep this discrepancy open under alignment D01/D02 until the product owner approves one rule.
2. **New attempt policy:** repeated enrollment of the same Student and scenario version returns the existing enrollment. UC-05 alternative 1a mentions “Start a New Attempt,” but no general UI/API policy currently implements it.
3. **Asynchronous practical evaluation:** current code supports objective quiz results and preserves unavailable evidence, but does not provide the UC prose's general asynchronous grading flow for open/practical artifacts.
4. **Human acceptance:** automated source/test trace is available; current browser UAT and human reviewer sign-off for the full US-05/US-06 journey remain pending.

## Recommended UAT set

- Start a published simulation, save a draft, reload the browser and confirm restoration.
- Retry the same finalize request and confirm only one FINAL submission exists.
- Confirm the model answer is denied before FINAL and available afterward.
- Verify the next task remains locked until self-review.
- Complete a quiz with score `0` and confirm progression is governed by review, not score.
- Complete all required work and verify statuses, objective-only score display, practiced skills, completion wording and owner-only access from a second account.
