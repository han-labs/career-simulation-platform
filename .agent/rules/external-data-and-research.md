# External Data and Research Rules

## Approved academic purpose

The project lead has approved external-data research for this non-commercial course project. A collector may retrieve permitted, publicly accessible factual metadata for IT-related simulations from The Forage when the current Terms of Use, `robots.txt`, and other source controls allow automated access.

Non-commercial academic purpose is the reason for collection; it is not by itself a copyright license or permission to ignore source terms. If current permission cannot be verified, the collector must stop and the product must continue with synthetic or original content.

## The Forage reference boundary

Allowed when current source controls permit it:

- study public navigation, discovery, filters, metadata, progression, and reflection patterns;
- document high-level product concepts in your own words;
- create original tasks inspired by real job responsibility categories.
- collect public factual catalog metadata needed to find and compare IT simulations: canonical source URL, simulation title, publisher/employer attribution, public category/track, difficulty, duration, task count, and skill labels;
- normalize those facts into CareerSim categories for research and draft review, while retaining source attribution and retrieval date.

Not allowed without separate written permission or a compatible license:

- copying employer task descriptions, files, model answers, certificates, branding, screenshots, or datasets;
- crawling behind login or bypassing rate limits/technical controls;
- republishing protected scraped content or presenting source text as CareerSim-authored content;
- presenting The Forage or an employer as a project partner.

## Collector controls

- Check and record the applicable Terms of Use and `robots.txt` immediately before every collection run; do not rely on an old approval record.
- Use only public, non-authenticated pages and never bypass CAPTCHA, access controls, blocked routes, or anti-automation measures.
- Use a descriptive user agent, cache fetched pages, run sequentially, and default to no more than one request per second unless the source explicitly permits more.
- Collect no student/user profiles, reviews, email addresses, submitted work, certificates, tracking identifiers, or other personal data.
- Store source URL, retrieval timestamp, and source terms/robots review with every imported record.
- Imported records enter a non-public review state. A human must verify provenance, factual accuracy, allowed fields, and removal/update procedure before use.
- Treat all external descriptions as research input. CareerSim simulation instructions, tasks, answer keys, evaluation rules, and media must remain original or separately licensed.
- Fail closed: a denied response, terms ambiguity, robots restriction, markup change, or missing provenance stops that source instead of triggering a bypass.

## Source record

Every external dataset/content source must record:

```text
Source URL:
Publisher:
Retrieved date:
License/terms:
Permitted project use:
Fields/content used:
Transformations:
Reviewer:
Removal/update procedure:
```

If licensing or provenance is unclear, use synthetic/original content until the team approves the source.
